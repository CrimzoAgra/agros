# The desktop

## The bar

The bar runs down the left edge of the screen. From top to bottom:

- **The AgrOS logo**
- **Workspaces**: the dots. Hover one to preview it, click to go there
- **The dock**: pinned apps and the ones that are open. Minimised windows are dimmed. Right-click an app to pin it,
  unpin it or force-quit it
- **The tray**: click the arrow to open it
- **The clock**, then **status icons** (sound, network, Bluetooth, battery) that open their panels on hover
- **The power button**: lock, log out, restart, shut down

Right-click an empty part of the bar for the Task Manager and the floating/tiling switch.

## The app menu

Press **Super**, type, and press Enter. Pinned favourites come first, then every app from A to Z. The button next to
the search bar checks for updates.

## Notifications

Pop-ups appear in the corner. To see older ones, move the pointer to the top edge of the screen above them, or press
**Super+K**. Click a notification to go to the app that sent it. **Ctrl+Alt+C** clears them all.

## Desktop widgets

Behind your windows sit a clock, the weather with a 3-day forecast, a calendar, system meters and a music player. To set
the weather's town, hover the town name on the weather panel, click the pencil, type, and pick it from the list.

## Wallpaper and colours

Open the app menu and type `>wallpaper` (the `>` starts a command), or use Caelestia's dashboard. Colours across the
desktop, Discord and Spotify follow the wallpaper.

## Lock screen

**Super+L** locks the screen. It also locks itself before the computer goes to sleep.
