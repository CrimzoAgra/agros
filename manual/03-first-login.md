# Your first login

Log in on the AgrOS login screen. Your first login takes a few minutes longer than usual, because **AgrOS Welcome**
finishes the parts that belong to your account:

- the wallpaper and the colours taken from it
- LibreWolf's settings and its Lagoon theme
- Spotify, Nuvio and ArtCraft
- VSCodium's extensions and Claude Code's plugins

You can use the desktop while it works. If a step fails (no internet, for example), it simply tries again at your next
login.

## Two buttons to know about

When it's done, a notification offers two things:

- **Restore my accounts**: plug in your pen drive and click it to bring back your logins (Discord, Spotify, Steam,
  your browser profile...). No pen drive? It opens each app for you to sign in instead. See
  [Your accounts](13-accounts.md).
- **Install hardware apps and VMware**: installs the apps for the devices you have plugged in: Razer, Logitech, Moza
  wheels, drawing tablets. It also installs VMware Workstation.

Missed the notification? Run `agros-personal` (accounts) or `agros-welcome --system` (hardware apps) in a terminal.

## Finding your way around

- Press **Super** (the Windows key) to open the app menu and type to search.
- The bar on the left holds your workspaces, the dock, the tray, the clock and the power button.
- **Super+T** opens a terminal, **Super+W** the browser, **Super+E** your files.
