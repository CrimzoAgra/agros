# Windows and workspaces

## Windows

Windows behave the way they do on Windows:

- New windows open in the middle of the screen at a standard size. Several in a row cascade down and to the right.
- Floating windows have a title bar with **minimise**, **maximise** and **close**. Double-click the title bar to
  maximise; drag a maximised window to un-maximise it.
- Resize by dragging any edge or corner.
- Minimised windows stay in the dock, dimmed. Click them, or press Alt+Tab, to bring them back.

### Floating or tiling

Press **Super+Alt+Space** (or right-click an empty part of the bar) to switch between:

- **Floating**: windows sit wherever you put them, like on Windows. This is the default.
- **Tiling**: windows fill the screen side by side and rearrange themselves as you open and close them.

## Workspaces

Workspaces are like Windows' virtual desktops. You have ten: **Super+1** to **Super+0** jumps between them, and the dots
at the top of the bar show which ones hold windows. Hover a dot for a live preview.

On a laptop, swipe sideways on the touchpad to move between them.

### More than one screen

Each screen has its own ten workspaces, and they move together: Super+3 shows workspace 3 on every screen. When you
unplug a screen, its windows move to the screen that's left; plug it back in and they go home.

On a laptop with another screen connected, closing the lid turns the laptop's own screen off instead of sleeping.
