# Welcome to AgrOS

AgrOS is a complete desktop that installs in one go. Under the hood it's [CachyOS](https://cachyos.org), a fast,
gaming-friendly version of Arch Linux. On top of that, AgrOS adds a finished desktop: nothing to configure, nothing
to piece together.

## What you get

- **A modern desktop**: [Hyprland](https://hyprland.org) with the [Caelestia](https://github.com/caelestia-dots/shell)
  shell. A bar down the left side with a dock, a Super-key menu for apps, notifications, desktop widgets (clock,
  weather, calendar, system meters, music), and colours taken from your wallpaper.
- **Windows-style habits**: windows float and open in the middle, with title bars, minimise and maximise.
  Alt+Tab switches windows, and Ctrl+Shift+Esc opens a task manager.
- **Your apps, installed**: a browser, Discord, Spotify, an office suite, an image editor, a code editor and more. See
  [Apps](08-apps.md).
- **Gaming**: Steam, Lutris (one library for Steam, EA, Ubisoft, Epic, GOG, Battle.net and Rockstar), Proton,
  MangoHud and Gamescope. See [Gaming](09-gaming.md).
- **Updates that look after themselves**: everything is updated at boot and installed when you shut down. See
  [Updates](12-updates.md).

## What it isn't

AgrOS holds none of your personal data. Logins, browser profiles and keys stay yours: you carry them on an encrypted
pen drive and restore them after installing (see [Your accounts](13-accounts.md)).
